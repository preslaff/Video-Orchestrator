# Concept Scenarios – Video Greetings & Product Ads

Format for each scenario: occasion → style → length → beats (scenes) → music → on-screen text → what the client provides.
Timings are approximate; each beat = 1–2 generated clips.

---

## 1. "The Years in One Day" – adult birthday (cinematic)

- **Occasion:** birthday, ages 30–70
- **Style:** realistic, warm film lighting, shallow depth of field
- **Length:** 45–60 sec
- **Beats:**
  1. Sunrise, empty kitchen, a cup of coffee – "One day. One life."
  2. Childhood memories – an old photo comes alive (image-to-video), summer, a bicycle
  3. Youth – first job, first love, city in motion
  4. Today – family around the table, candles, laughter
  5. Finale: the birthday person alone, looking out a window, a smile – frame freezes into a "photograph"
- **Music:** piano building into strings
- **Text:** name + age in beat 1; closing "Happy birthday, [name]. The best is yet to come."
- **From the client:** 5–8 photos from different periods, 2–3 life facts

## 2. "The Little Hero" – children's birthday (animation)

- **Occasion:** child's birthday, ages 3–10
- **Style:** Pixar-style 3D or watercolor 2D – the child is the main character
- **Length:** 30–45 sec
- **Beats:**
  1. The child (animated character based on a photo) wakes up – today is special
  2. Adventure in their favorite theme (dinosaurs, space, princesses, football)
  3. Encounter with the "guardian of the cake" – a humorous obstacle
  4. All friends/family appear as animated characters
  5. The cake, the candles, hands in the air – "Happy [N]th birthday!"
- **Music:** playful orchestral, percussion hits on every twist
- **Text:** name and age as a "movie title" at the start
- **From the client:** photo of the child, favorite theme/character, names of 3–4 loved ones

## 3. "Your Name Carries a Story" – name day (animation / art)

- **Occasion:** name day (George, Dimitar, Nicholas, John, Mary…)
- **Style:** artistic – an icon/fresco coming alive as stylized animation, then transitioning to contemporary art
- **Length:** 30–40 sec
- **Beats:**
  1. Candle, old parchment – the name written in calligraphy
  2. The saint's legend in 2–3 stylized shots (e.g. St. George and the dragon)
  3. Transition: the saint's features "melt" into today's person
  4. A toast, family, sunshine
- **Music:** acoustic guitar/bagpipe evolving into a modern arrangement
- **Text:** meaning of the name + "Happy name day, [name]!"
- **From the client:** photo, name, one character trait to tie into the legend

## 4. "Two Rivers" – wedding (cinematic)

- **Occasion:** wedding / gift from best man, maid of honor, or parents
- **Style:** realistic, cinematic, golden hour, slow motion
- **Length:** 60–90 sec
- **Beats:**
  1. Parallel edit – the two of them separately as children (photos come alive)
  2. Parallel continues – youth, different cities/paths
  3. First meeting – their place (café, park, workplace), restyled
  4. Together – travels, home, small everyday moments
  5. The wedding day – dress, rings, first dance (if footage exists) or a symbolic shot
  6. Finale: two rivers merging into one – wide drone shot
- **Music:** strings + wordless vocals, climax at beat 6
- **Text:** names as the title; at the end the wedding date and "One story. One future."
- **From the client:** 10+ photos of the couple, how they met in 3 sentences

## 5. "Fifty More" – wedding anniversary (animation with real photos)

- **Occasion:** 10th / 25th / 50th anniversary
- **Style:** collage animation – real photos inside a hand-drawn world (paper-cut / stop-motion feel)
- **Length:** 45–60 sec
- **Beats:**
  1. A calendar with pages flipping back to the wedding year
  2. The wedding photo "comes alive" – subtle movement, confetti
  3. Timeline – each decade = one scene: home, children, grandchildren
  4. Today – the two on the sofa, hand in hand
  5. The calendar flips forward – blank pages, "fifty more"
- **Music:** waltz, retro melody from the wedding year
- **Text:** wedding year → current year; "[N] years. One love."
- **From the client:** wedding photo + 1–2 photos per decade

## 6. "The First Day After the Last" – graduation (cinematic)

- **Occasion:** high school / university graduation
- **Style:** realistic, dynamic, coming-of-age film – lens flares, light handheld camera
- **Length:** 40–50 sec
- **Beats:**
  1. Empty classroom/lecture hall – light through the windows
  2. Fast montage: textbooks, sleepless nights, coffee, laptop, friends
  3. The diploma moment – cap in the air (freeze frame)
  4. A door opens – road, city, horizon
  5. Finale: diploma in hand + a gesture from loved ones
- **Music:** indie/electronic with a bass drop on beat 3
- **Text:** name + field of study; "This is not the end. This is the first page."
- **From the client:** graduation photo, field of study, dream direction

## 7. "Roots" – family story (documentary / cinematic)

- **Occasion:** gift for parents/grandparents, family reunion
- **Style:** documentary, warm, archival aesthetic (grain, sepia → color)
- **Length:** 90–150 sec
- **Beats:**
  1. A family tree growing from the ground – names appear on the branches
  2. The place of origin – village/town, the house, the fields (generated from description)
  3. Each generation = one chapter: photo comes alive + 2–3 facts as subtitles
  4. Turning points: weddings, births, migration
  5. Today – everyone together (photo from a family gathering)
  6. The tree again – with new branches for the youngest
- **Music:** folk motifs reworked with modern strings
- **Text:** names and years per chapter; finale "This is where we come from. Because of you, we carry on."
- **From the client:** photos by generation, short stories/facts, name of the place of origin

## 8. "Highlight of a Life" – milestone jubilee 50/60/70 (cinematic, epic)

- **Occasion:** round-number birthday, celebrated with many guests
- **Style:** movie trailer – epic, dark background, gold titles
- **Length:** 60 sec (for screening at the party itself)
- **Beats:**
  1. Black screen, voice/text: "Every legend has a beginning" – year of birth
  2. Quick cuts: childhood, youth, career – each with a "credit" like a film role
  3. Humorous "achievements" (from tips given by loved ones)
  4. The family – "the supporting cast"
  5. Finale: the name as a movie title + "Season 2 starts now"
- **Music:** epic trailer, drums, climax
- **Text:** styled as film credits
- **From the client:** photos, 5 fun facts, profession, family names

## 9. "The Promise" – product ad (cinematic, 15–30 sec)

- **Occasion:** any product where visuals sell – perfume, cosmetics, food/drinks, jewelry, furniture
- **Style:** studio, macro, slow motion, controlled lighting; one "hero shot" at the end
- **Length:** 15 sec (social) / 30 sec (website)
- **Beats (universal formula):**
  1. Atmosphere – the feeling the product promises (perfume = evening in the city; coffee = morning; jewelry = light on skin)
  2. Detail – macro texture: a drop, steam, metal, fabric
  3. Use – a hand, a gesture, a moment of pleasure
  4. Hero shot – the product alone, centered, logo
- **Music:** minimalist, one accent on beat 4
- **Text:** slogan + brand only, in beat 4
- **From the client:** product photos, slogan, 3 words describing the feeling
- **Variants by category:**
  - Perfume – night, silk, city lights, glass with reflections
  - Food/drink – steam, drops, a "break/crack" moment, appetizing lighting
  - Jewelry – skin, light glints, slow rotation
  - Furniture – empty room → furnished, daylight
  - Service – problem (gray) → solution (color), 3 shots
